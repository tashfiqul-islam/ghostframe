## F. Scenario mocks

Full screens, 43 scenarios: v6's 30, each redrawn for Cockpit (F.1 to F.30), and the 13 the new structure needs (F.31 to F.43). Each is exactly the stated columns × rows, with the prompt box, hint line and the 2-row status line at the bottom, as in fullscreen mode.

How the screens are laid out. Docked screens are 200 columns unless stated: the transcript region is columns 0 to 125 (frame 0 to 119), the engine's divider is column 126, the pane's body is columns 128 to 199. The pane's first row is the engine's own, with its close mark. The prompt box, the hint line and the status line span the full width under both halves; the left now-row sits at the bottom of the transcript region. Fallback screens are 100 or 60 columns and have no pane. Hero mocks carry `paint` and `bg` blocks; the others have a short legend.

Which mode each scenario is drawn in:

| Drawn docked (200) | Drawn in fallback (100 or 60) | Drawn in both |
|---|---|---|
| F.1 to F.4, F.7, F.8, F.10, F.12 to F.14, F.17 to F.27, F.29, F.30, F.32 to F.38, F.41, F.43 | F.5, F.6, F.9, F.11, F.15, F.16, F.28 | F.31, F.39, F.40, F.42 |

Where a v6 scenario and a new one cover the same event (a 300-step turn, seven agents, a permission prompt, plan mode, compaction, resume), the v6 number shows it in fallback and the new number shows it docked, so every one of them exists in both modes.
