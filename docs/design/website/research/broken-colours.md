Conclusion for the broken ones:

- All 44 broken ruler patterns have the minor-tick geometry: a 1 px tick every 8 px across, or every 16 px down.
- Intact patterns with that geometry use one of two colours. The 10 on the page canvas all use `#2A2A34`. The 14 that
  use `rgba(236,234,242,.3)` all sit inside the hero Frame overlay, and none of the broken ones are in a Frame.
- The one broken 5 px / 8 px pattern (Boot #0) is a meter track under a `#7EC8BE` fill. All 44 intact meter tracks
  use `#2A2A34`.
- Rule 2 therefore holds for every tick pattern it is applied to below. It would be wrong for major ticks (`#3A3A47`)
  and for in-Frame ticks, but no broken value is one of those.

Surface-1 card borders: no intact border exists on these exact components, because every instance is broken. Rule 2
rests on the owner's rule and on `tokens.css` (`--line-2` "borders"). The closest intact surface-1 element, the nav
indicator `background:#111117;outline:1px solid #2A2A34`, agrees.
